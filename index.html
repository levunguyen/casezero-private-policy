Privacy Policy for Case Zero

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Case Zero ("the App") handles information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Case Zero is designed primarily as a mobile detective game. We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, home address, phone number, or government identification number through the game itself.

Depending on the version of the App and the services enabled, certain technical information may be processed automatically by third-party services used by the App, such as advertising and crash/error monitoring services.

2. Advertising

Case Zero may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect or use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information about ad interactions in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices here: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Crash Reports and Error Monitoring

Case Zero may use Sentry to help us identify crashes, errors, and technical problems and improve the stability of the App.

Sentry may process technical information such as device information, operating system information, application version, error information, and diagnostic data associated with an error.

For more information, please see: Sentry Privacy Policy .

4. How We Use Information

Information processed through the App or its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the game;
  • Improve game performance and user experience;
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes and technical problems;
  • Display and measure advertisements where advertising is enabled;
  • Protect the App against abuse, fraud, or technical attacks.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when this is necessary to provide advertising, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, or other technical services used by the App. These providers process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

6. Children's Privacy

Case Zero is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You may be able to control certain advertising and privacy settings through your device settings and through settings provided by the advertising provider.

You may also stop the collection of information by uninstalling the App.

9. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies. These may include:

  • Google AdMob – advertising services
  • Sentry – crash and error monitoring

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
